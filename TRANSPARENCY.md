# 📜 TRANSPARENCY.md — EU AI Act Compliance

**Law over code — القانون فوق الكود.** Technology serves the legal and civic framework, never the other way around.

- **Regime:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act), **Article 50 transparency obligations — effective 2 August 2026**
- **Repository:** `teos-dealmaker` (TEOS DealMaker) v1.1.0
- **Positioning:** self-hosted by default; data, code, and credentials stay inside the customer's perimeter
- **Method:** every obligation below is enforced in code at the point where content is exposed to a natural person — not as a UI patch. This file records **what exists**, with file references an auditor can open. It does not claim capabilities that are not implemented.

## 🔑 Obligations & Enforcement

### 1. AI identification — Art. 50(1)

- **Requirement:** Chatbots and interactive AI systems must clearly identify themselves as AI when interacting with a natural person.
- **Applies to this product:** **Yes** — the TEOS DealMaker bot (`bot/index.js`) interacts with humans on Telegram.
- **Enforcement (implemented):**
  - `services/transparency.js` defines the single source-of-truth disclosure in English and Arabic (`withAiDisclosure`).
  - `bot/index.js:8` imports the disclosure helper; `bot/index.js:30` passes **every** reply the assistant sends through `withAiDisclosure(result.text, i18n.getLang(...))` before `sendMessage`. The disclosure is appended in the send path, so no bot message can be dispatched unmarked. Re-applying is idempotent (never duplicates the footer).
  - The disclosure is language-aware: `🤖 This message was generated by an AI assistant.` (EN) / `🤖 هذه الرسالة من إعداد مساعد ذكاء اصطناعي.` (AR).
- **Guarantee:** the marker is applied in the single `sendMessage` call site — a message that did not carry the disclosure would have to bypass the send path entirely.

### 2. Content marking — Art. 50(2)

- **Requirement:** AI-generated or AI-altered content exposed to the public / a natural person must be marked in a machine-readable format and detectable as artificially generated.
- **Applies to this product:** **Yes** — the governed outbound workforce composes emails delivered to prospects/customers.
- **Enforcement (implemented):**
  - `services/transparency.js` defines the machine-readable marker `AI-CONTENT-MARKER: AI-Generated Content` plus a human-readable disclosure line (`withContentMarking`).
  - `services/outboundWorker/index.js:27` imports the helper; `services/outboundWorker/index.js:379` sends every job's email as `text: withContentMarking(job.body)`. The marker is appended in the send path, so an email can never leave the perimeter unmarked. Idempotent.
  - `services/outboundWorker/index.js:400` records `content_marked: 'ai-generated'` in the `EMAIL_JOB_SENT` audit entry for traceability.
- **Scope note:** outbound remains **PAUSED / DISABLED** in production by default; resume requires explicit founder action. When it runs, marking is guaranteed at the send boundary.

### 3. Deepfake labelling — Art. 50(3)

- **Requirement:** AI-generated or manipulated image, audio, or video content must be clearly labelled as deepfakes.
- **Applies to this product:** **Not applicable** — TEOS DealMaker does not generate or manipulate image, audio, or video content. No media synthesis capability exists in this codebase.
- **Control:** if media generation is ever added, Art. 50(3) becomes applicable and must be enforced before exposure; this is tracked in `deliverables/ROADMAP.md`.

### 4. Emotion recognition & biometric categorisation disclosure — Art. 50(4)

- **Requirement:** Deployers of emotion recognition or biometric categorisation systems must inform the natural persons exposed.
- **Applies to this product:** **Not applicable** — TEOS DealMaker does not perform emotion recognition or biometric categorisation. No such capability exists in this codebase.
- **Control:** adding either capability makes Art. 50(4) applicable and requires an operator disclosure flow before any exposure; tracked in `deliverables/ROADMAP.md`.

## 🛡️ Architecture Guarantees

- **Send-path enforcement:** both applicable obligations are enforced in the exact code path that exposes content to a natural person (`bot/index.js` `sendMessage`; `services/outboundWorker` `sendRaw`). There is no separate "compliance mode" that can be switched off.
- **Fail-closed posture:** the system's outbound worker refuses to send without verification, approval, and policy PASS (see `services/outboundWorker/index.js`); transparency marking is applied at that same boundary.
- **Deterministic + auditable:** markers are produced by `services/transparency.js`, covered by `tests/test-transparency.js`, and enforced by `scripts/transparency-check.js` (CI + pre-push hook via `npm run hooks:install`).
- **Self-hosting:** data, code, and credentials remain within the customer's perimeter by design; no third-party processing of customer data is required for these obligations.
- **Operational sovereignty:** customers control the deployment, the model providers, and the governance policies governing their instance.
