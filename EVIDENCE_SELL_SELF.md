# TEOS DealMaker Self-Sell Mission Evidence

## Demonstration Summary

**TEOS DealMaker can run a governed sales mission for itself.**  
It takes a business objective, coordinates a specialized AI workforce, researches the market, defines the ideal customer profile, analyzes competition, develops positioning and pricing, prioritizes target accounts, reaches a governance checkpoint, and completes the mission with an actionable sales strategy.

**The demonstrated capability is governed AI execution—not autonomous selling.**

**Law over Code. Evidence over Claims.**

## Detailed Evidence

### What Was Demonstrated

1. **Governed Mission Orchestration**
   - Policy evaluation executed before every capability invocation
   - Workflow adhered to deny-wins policy enforcement model
   - Mission Controller orchestrated execution via Workforce Runtime

2. **Multi-Agent AI Workforce Coordination** (6 specialized agents)
   - Revenue Strategist: Mission viability assessment & workforce planning
   - Intelligence Agent: Competitive research using internal company knowledge
   - Market Intelligence: Target industry research & ICP development
   - Strategist: Tactical positioning & pricing strategy development
   - Revenue Strategist: Target account prioritization & pipeline estimation
   - Gatekeeper: Final strategy review against safety & policy constraints

3. **Complete Mission Lifecycle**
   - Mission initialized: Workspace, founder user, knowledge base created
   - 6 mission steps executed in coordinated sequence
   - Governance checkpoint reached: Mission entered `waiting_approval` state
   - Approval processed: Founder approval simulated in test environment
   - Workflow resumed: Final gatekeeper step executed
   - Mission completed: All steps marked `COMPLETED`, status: `completed`

4. **Actionable Output Generated**
   - Executive briefing summarizing all agent findings
   - Positioning recommendations based on market analysis
   - Pricing strategy derived from competitive positioning
   - Prioritized target account list with engagement sequencing
   - Pipeline estimate for revenue forecasting

### What Was NOT Demonstrated

- Real outbound communication execution (service remained PAUSED/DISABLED)
- External prospect contact (no emails/messages sent to actual recipients)
- CRM deal creation (no deals staged in pipeline)
- Customer acquisition (no external engagements initiated)
- Revenue generation (no actual sales transactions occurred)
- Independent human founder approval (approval was test-simulated)
